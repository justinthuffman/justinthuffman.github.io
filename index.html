About

I'm a technology consultant who sits between the server room and the boardroom. I turn business needs into technical plans, and technical work into summaries that leaders can act on.

I've been hooked on technology since I took apart my parents' brand-new Betamax at age eight to see how it worked. Building a PC to play Doom in 1994 led to wiring up LAN parties in the late '90s. That grew into a career building and running networks, servers, websites, and software.

Today I run Huffman Information Technologies, where I serve as a virtual CIO and hands-on engineer for my clients. I still love to tinker, whether that means benchmarking a PC or wiring an Arduino to open the garage door from my phone. Away from screens, you'll find me spinning records, enjoying a craft brew, or looking for the next great place to eat around Columbus.
